# IPL Analytics – 2008 to 2024

## 📌 Overview
This project explores **IPL match and player statistics** from 2008–2024 using interactive dashboards. It highlights batting, bowling, and team performance trends, offering insights into runs, wickets, strike rates, match outcomes, and venue history.

---

## 🎯 Project Goals
- Analyze IPL matches and deliveries across seasons.  
- Track batting and bowling performance by player and team.  
- Visualize tournament trends: runs, wickets, strike rates, economy.  
- Provide recruiters and analysts with clear insights into player impact and team success.  

---

## 📝 Problem Statement
1. Collect and preprocess IPL match and ball‑by‑ball data.  
2. Build dashboards for batting, bowling, and overall tournament summaries.  
3. Identify standout players, winning strategies, and venue patterns.  
4. Deliver actionable insights for sports analytics and decision‑making.  

---

## 📊 Data Source
- **ipl_2008-2024 matches.csv** → Match‑level data (teams, venues, results, player of match).  
- **ipl_2008-2024 deliveries.csv** → Ball‑by‑ball data (runs, wickets, extras, player actions).  

---

## 🔍 Dashboard Highlights
- **Batsman Stats** – Runs, strike rates, dismissals, economy vs batsmen.  
- **Bowling Stats** – Wickets, averages, strike rates, economy by bowler/team.  
- **IPL Summary** – Tournament totals, match outcomes, team wins, venues, player of match awards.  

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Top performers**: AB de Villiers, Chris Gayle, Rohit Sharma, Virat Kohli, MS Dhoni consistently dominate player‑of‑match awards.  
- **Winning strategies**: Teams batting second (fielding first) win ~64% of matches.  
- **Match outcomes**: Majority of wins come by wickets rather than runs.  
- **Venue impact**: Wankhede Stadium and Sawai Mansingh Stadium host the most matches.  

---

## 🚀 Recommendations
- Use dashboards to **scout high‑impact players** for recruitment.  
- Analyze **venue‑specific performance** to optimize team strategies.  
- Track **batting strike rates and bowling economy** for balanced team selection.
